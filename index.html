Specialize, or fuse?

A golden age of enchanted engines ended in catastrophe. Now the Aether is running out, the borders are a standoff, and every nation has its own answer to the question the war is fought over.

Synergy

The Concord

Magic and machine braided together. Runed steel, blessed armour, arc-fire from the line. The balanced hand that can answer anything.

Tech supremacy

The Directive

Brass, steam and a rigid economy. Pike walls, rifle lines and iron engines. Order is the weapon.

Magic

The Fervent

Faith turned to fire. A volatile swarm that burns bright and hits like a storm, if it lives long enough.

The wildcard

The Forewarned

Sacrifice and experiment, whatever it costs. Bone, black iron and things that should not walk. The dark horse of the war.
